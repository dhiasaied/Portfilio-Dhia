01. About me

Developer mindset, designer's eye.

I'm Dhia Eddine Saied, a Full Stack Developer from Nabeul, Tunisia, with a Higher Technician Diploma in Web Development and hands-on experience designing, building and maintaining web applications, from RESTful APIs and MVC back ends to component-driven SPA front ends.

My stack spans Angular, React, Symfony, Node.js / Express.js and Flutter, backed by MySQL, SQL Server, SQLite and Firebase. On the design side, I prototype complete product experiences in Figma, including dashboards, design systems and mobile flows, before turning them into production-ready code.

I care about clean, maintainable and documented code, web security (SQL-injection and CSRF prevention), performance, and accessible interfaces. I'm used to working independently in distributed teams with Git/GitHub, Scrum/Kanban and asynchronous communication.
